How to play Handland

Goal: be the first to collect the chosen number of Monopoly tokens (1, 2 or 3). You score a token by holding 5 of the same resource for a full round.

What you have

  • Resources (5 slots): Oil, Livestock, Farmland, Weapons, Workers. Other players see only the backs.
  • Hand (3 cards): Action cards (one-shot) and Boost cards (stay on the table).
  • Heat and Cover tokens, which track how your call-outs have gone.

Your turn

  1. You automatically refill any empty resource slots and draw 1 hand card.
  2. In any order: offer one trade and play cards.
  3. End your turn, discarding down to your slot and hand limits. The only ways to change your resources are trading, paying for cards, and winning call-outs.

Trading (face down)

You put a card face down and say what it is. You may lie. The other player puts a card down and says what theirs is. Either side may call scam on the other:

  • They lied → the caller keeps both cards.
  • They were honest → the accused keeps both cards, and the caller takes a penalty.

If they say they are giving something other than what you asked for, you may also call the trade off: both cards go back. A card put into a trade leaves its place in your row empty and returns to that same place.

Paying for cards (also face down)

Most cards cost resources. You pay face down and say you paid the right thing. Anyone may call your bluff. Caught lying → your card is wasted and the caller takes your payment. Wrongly accused → your card works and the caller takes a penalty. Paying is also how you get rid of resources you don't want.

Heat and Cover

  • Wrong call-out: lose 1 Cover. No Cover? Gain 1 Heat.
  • Right call-out: lose 1 Heat. No Heat? Gain 1 Cover (max 3).
  • Each Heat (max 5) turns one of your resource cards face up for everyone, starting from the left.
  • At 5 Heat with no Cover, a wrong call destroys your leftmost card instead.

Monopolies

End your turn with 5 of a kind and a secret one-round hold begins. Nobody is told. If you still have all 5 at the start of your next turn, you reveal your cards, score 1 Monopoly token, discard those 5 and draw 5 new ones. Tokens are score only. If a trade, a Forced Trade or a lost call-out drops you below 5 at any point in the round, the hold is broken. Heat is dangerous here: face-up cards show everyone what you are collecting.

Monopoly powers

When you score a monopoly you may use that resource's power once, right then, before anything else on your turn. It is free, so nobody can call it. The winning token has no power: the game is over.
